About this website

This is a page which introduces the creature lookup website

This website was created with the idea of providing a simple lookup tool for people who are interested in creatures and visitors to natural museums, especially those who want to learn more about different species.

Information about the animals is presented in a concise, easy to read format for people of all ages.
